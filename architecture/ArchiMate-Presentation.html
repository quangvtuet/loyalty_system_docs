Technology & Infrastructure Layer (Simplified 4-Tier View)Technology & Infrastructure
Loading diagram...
Scroll to Zoom · Drag to Pan

1. Edge Ingress & Gateway

Ingress Controller validates JWT, enforces OAuth 2.0 rate limits (1,000 req/min/partner), and routes traffic.

2. Kubernetes Pods Platform

5 Microservices containerized on K8s cluster with Horizontal Pod Autoscaler (HPA) scaling up to ≥ 500 TPS.

3. Middleware Kafka & Redis

Kafka 3-Broker Cluster partitioned by member_id; Redis Sentinel provides Redlock and 72-hour idempotency cache.

4. Polyglot Storage & CDC

4 isolated PostgreSQL DBs for transactional integrity + ClickHouse Star Schema synced via Debezium CDC.
